# Changelog

## 0.1.0

1 November 2026

The first release of the local editor. A venue stays in `venue.json` on your machine.

- Create a project, draw walls, rooms, doors, and furniture, and undo those edits.
- The plan and the 3D hall stay in step. Orbit, Isometric, Walk, and Map are included.
- Map can list the named rooms and tables and open a card for the one you choose. Chairs stay off that list.
- Thirty generated models ship with the editor. Each one is Apache-2.0.
- Export writes JSON, an SVG or PNG plan, a one-page PDF, or a GLB.
- Projects stay in this browser and save again after an edit.
- Checks and capacity are planning aids. They are not a safety certification or an approved occupancy number.
- The editor can be used from the keyboard. Imported JSON and GLB files are checked before they load.

Not in this release: the visitor app, cloud sync, a menu on a zone, walking to a chosen place, and stairs between floors. The plan editor still edits the ground floor.
