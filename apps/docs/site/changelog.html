0.1.0

1 November 2026. The first release of the local editor.

  • Create a project, draw walls, rooms, doors, and furniture, and undo those edits.
  • The plan and the 3D hall stay in step. Orbit, Isometric, Walk, and Map are included.
  • Map can list the named rooms and tables and open a card for the one you choose.
  • Thirty Apache-2.0 models ship with the editor.
  • Export writes JSON, an SVG or PNG plan, a one-page PDF, or a GLB.
  • Projects stay in this browser.
  • Checks and capacity are planning aids, not a certification.
  • The editor can be used from the keyboard, and imported files are checked before they load.

Not in 0.1.0: the visitor app, cloud sync, a menu on a zone, walking to a chosen place, and stairs between floors.
